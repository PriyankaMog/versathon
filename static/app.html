    Coverage area

    Wards are placed around one point. Set it to the centre of your city or district, so residents who share their location are matched to the nearest ward. Existing reports move with the wards.

    Ward names and boundaries are placeholders until real ward data is connected.

    Presentation mode: outbreak simulator

    Available only because the server was started with OR_DEMO=1. Simulated reports are labelled and go through the same detection code as real ones.

    "Test spam accounts" creates two temporary accounts that send 15 reports. The system holds back the alert, flags the main account, and you can suspend it under Moderation.
